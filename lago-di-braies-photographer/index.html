Planning & access

Planning & the best light at Lago di Braies

Lago di Braies is protected, and access is regulated — planning around it is exactly what gets you calm, glowing images instead of crowds.

Driving in summer. From 1 July to 15 September, cars can only reach the lake between 09:00 and 16:00, and only with an advance online reservation. Outside that window — early morning and evening — the road is open.

Why that's good news for your photos. It points us straight to the best light anyway: before 09:00 (sunrise) and after 16:00 into sunset. That's when the water is still, the peaks glow, and the lake is quietest — so that's when I plan your session.

The historic rowing boats. The wooden boats run seasonally (late April to early November), widest hours in high summer. If the boathouse is part of your vision, we time the shoot to the boat hours.

Booking. Shooting slots at the boathouse can be reserved from 2 February each year — book early for peak dates.

Drones. As of 2026, drones are strictly prohibited at the lake, monitored by rangers and fined — no aerial footage here.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you
need to know.

When is the best time for photos at Lago di Braies?

Around sunrise (before 09:00) or late afternoon into sunset (after 16:00) — softest light, calm water, fewest people. In summer (1 Jul–15 Sep) the daytime car restriction makes these hours the natural choice anyway.

Do we need a car reservation?

In summer (1 Jul–15 Sep), yes — to drive to the lake between 09:00 and 16:00 you need an online reservation. For sunrise or sunset sessions outside those hours it isn't required.

Can we use the rowing boats?

Yes, seasonally (roughly late April to early November; longest hours in high summer). We plan around the boat hours, and shooting slots are bookable from 2 February each year.

Can you fly a drone at the lake?

No — since 2026 drones are strictly banned at Lago di Braies and enforced by rangers.
